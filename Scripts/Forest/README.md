The files in this file are figures for the journal articles that are outputs from the script Scripts > N_Am_Meta_DHD_J-EPC0033278-QP-1-0_20260311_v04.R
These files were replaced by the figures in Scripts > HighDef because their resolution was not high enough for the journal.  These files are still used in draft of the article and are used as placeholders for the newer figures in HighDef.
The file is called forest because it was originally used just for the forest plot, before later being used for all graphs.

Journal_forestplot.png
Fig. 4.  Prevalence of microbes in animal fecal matter (95% confidence intervals).  Positive is how many samples detected positive for a microbe.  The number of reports for total does not equal the sum of other reports because multiple subgroup outcomes can be in a single report and outcomes for subgroups with fewer than 25 outcomes are not displayed.

Fig. 5.  Boxplots of generated microbe concentrations in animal feces from synthesized log-normal distributions in Table 1.  The y-axis has been log10 transformed and the horizontal lines on the graphs represents a concentration of 1.  Boxplots represent the median, interquartile range (25th to 75th percentiles), and whiskers the 95th percentile range.
